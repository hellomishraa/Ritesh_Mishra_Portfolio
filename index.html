About

Stories that feel as good as they look.

I’m a Delhi-NCR based filmmaker and visual storyteller creating films, digital content and visual stories across weddings, travel, documentaries and branded work.

I studied Information Technology at Manipal University Jaipur and built my craft alongside it, freelancing across cinematography, editing and storytelling. That work included a Government of India documentary and a film for Palace on Wheels, and I ran a small content agency shooting for cafés and salons.

Since joining Camlition Productions in September 2025, first as an intern and then full-time, I’ve gone deep into wedding filmmaking at destinations and premium venues — shooting, editing, teasers, reels and social content. My approach is cinematic, emotional and story-driven: strong compositions, movement, music and authentic moments.
